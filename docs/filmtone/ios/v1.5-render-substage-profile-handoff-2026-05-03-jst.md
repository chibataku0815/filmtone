- Among additive post-baseline stages, `GlowFamily` is the largest clear bottleneck, then `Vignette`, then `EdgeOptics`.

## Current Conclusion

Do not pursue quality mezzanine/decode first. The bottleneck remains Core Image render.

Optimization order:

1. `GlowFamily`: collapse/replace mip blur passes, gate no-op sub-branches, consider fewer mip levels or lower-res glow plates.
2. `Vignette`: optimize ray-angle vignette kernel or precompute/pass a cheaper mask path.
3. `EdgeOptics`: optimize RGB shift + edge softness, especially blur/ROI behavior.
4. `InputLut`/camera-profile base floor: investigate Apple Log input LUT cost separately with a cleaner A/B run, because current profiler cannot isolate it from source/base forced-render cost.
5. `Grain` and `CreativeLut`: lower priority.

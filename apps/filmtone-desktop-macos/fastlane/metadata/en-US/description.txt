Filmtone is a native macOS LUT editor for checking, adjusting, and exporting the color of videos and still images locally.

Choose a Look, tune Quick controls, switch Source Profile / Camera Conversion when needed, compare before and after, then export to a folder you select. The workflow stays local and is built for reviewing media before it leaves your desktop.

Key features:
- Still image and video preview
- Film Looks and LUT strength control
- Source Profile / Camera Conversion switching
- Before/After split comparison
- Video playback, rate control, and scrubbing
- Local export to a user-selected folder
- Sidecar JSON saved with exported media

No account, cloud sync, subscription, or AI generation is required. Open your material, make a color decision, and export the result from your Mac.

This update adds the foundation for highlight marker export flows, making it easier to carry selected moments into the next editing step.

Videos longer than five minutes now show a Desktop handoff sheet, so longer footage can move toward the Mac workflow when it needs a larger editing surface.

Cache management has been improved with a user-initiated cache release action and proactive low-disk cleanup for older temporary files.

Look and Backlight Veil energy merging now follows the same max-merge behavior as the macOS version, keeping color direction and veiling light more stable when both are active.

App Store metadata has also been reorganized for film grade, LUT, color, photo, and video discovery with less repetition across title, subtitle, and keyword fields.

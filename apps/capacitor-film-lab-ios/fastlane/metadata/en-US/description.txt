Filmtone helps you choose a film look, tune color with LUTs, add soft bloom, compare before/after, and export locally from iPhone to save or share.

Use it when the built-in editor feels too plain, but opening a full video editor feels too heavy. Filmtone keeps the color work local on iPhone.

What you can do:
- Pick media from the library
- Start from film-inspired presets and Filmtone Looks
- Adjust brightness, contrast, and saturation quickly
- Compare before and after
- Export on iPhone, then save to Photos or share

For more control, Filmtone supports two .cube LUT slots: Source Profile and Film Look. Use them for input transforms and creative looks while keeping the workflow local on iPhone.

Filmtone is not a capture camera. It is for finishing existing media: daily footage, travel footage, social posts, covers, thumbnails, and any set that should share the same color direction.

No account or cloud sync is required for the main workflow. Pick media, shape the color, export, save, and share from your iPhone.

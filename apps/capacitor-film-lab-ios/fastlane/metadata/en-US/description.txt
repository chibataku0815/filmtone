Filmtone is a local-first grading app for iPhone that lets you apply film looks to photos and short clips, then export right away.

Pick your source, choose a film stock preset, shape the look with the Quick 3 controls for exposure, contrast, and saturation, and add Camera Profile plus Film Look LUTs when you need a more precise setup. Check the compare preview, export, then save to Photos or share.

The core workflow stays on the device. Filmtone is built as a companion for fast local finishing without making account creation or cloud sync part of the main path.

The app is designed around H.264, HEVC, and ProRes source workflows so you can move from a quick local edit to a clean final export while keeping the Filmtone look intact.

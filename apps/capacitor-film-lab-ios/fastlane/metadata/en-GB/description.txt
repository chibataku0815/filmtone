Filmtone is an iPhone editor for choosing a film grade, tuning colour correction, shaping soft glow and grain, then exporting locally to save or share. Pick media, apply a built-in finish, adjust brightness, contrast, and saturation with Quick controls, compare before/after, and finish without leaving your iPhone.

Use Filmtone when the built-in editor feels too plain, but opening a full timeline-based video editing app feels too heavy. It is made for deciding a colour direction, checking the result, and exporting from iPhone without turning a simple finish into a large editing session.

The editor is a full-screen Liquid Glass workspace. Preview stays at the centre, while the bottom rail gives you a direct flow: choose a finish, adjust how strongly it applies, and check the result right away.

For more control, Filmtone supports two .cube LUT slots: Source Profile and Film Look. Source Profile is for input-side handling such as Apple Log, Apple Log 2, S-Log3, V-Log, and Rec.709. Film Look is for the final colour treatment. When you use camera-originated Log material or your own .cube LUT, you can keep the input transform and the finishing colour separate.

Use Filmtone for daily footage, travel footage, social posts, covers, thumbnails, LUT-based colour correction, and camera-originated Log workflows that still need to stay lightweight.

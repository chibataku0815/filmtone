Creative Look Pack previews now carry the selected Source Profile into the preview renderer. Stone and Urban on D-Log footage use the same input conversion in preview that export uses. Preview and export color handling were also aligned further, reducing cases where saved videos looked lifted or faded compared with the in-app preview.

Advanced controls now include Film Breath, Black Point, and Toe Contrast, so iPhone edits can tune motion variation, the black floor, and toe hardness.

24fps Slow Mode was added for supported videos, making slower 24fps exports easier to create from the iPhone workflow.

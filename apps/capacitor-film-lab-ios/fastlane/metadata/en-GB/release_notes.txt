This release restores audio through iPhone capture and normal video export. Filmtone records microphone audio for clips captured in the app, keeps audio from imported videos during normal exports, and checks the completed output file before treating audio as preserved. Highlight-reel exports remain silent by design.

Advanced controls now include Texture softness. It reduces hard fine detail and digital edge acutance separately from lens/periphery softness, with conservative source-aware tuning when metadata identifies iPhone, Apple Log, or similar sources.

The capture, editor, and export internals were also tightened, including stronger capture package persistence, sidecar audio diagnostics, and clearer export failures.

Filmtone is a large-screen editor for finishing colour while judging the result on a broad preview. It takes the Filmtone desktop editing model and adapts it to a touch-first workspace.

The preview stays central. Source, Look, Adjust, and Export stay in a persistent inspector, so you can open media, choose a Look, tune the result, compare before and after, and export without moving through a chain of modal screens.

Key features:
- Source: adjust input handling with Auto and built-in source profiles
- Look: apply Creative Pack 01, including Stone, Urban, and Noir; save, rename, favourite, and delete your own Looks
- Adjust: use Material Field, Backlight Veil, and Advanced Adjust from a large-screen control surface
- Preview: drag the still-image compare split, or toggle Compare during video playback
- Export: save to the photo library, share, or export to a file, with each destination showing whether media, sidecar, or package files are included

This edition is separate from the phone app. The phone app remains focused on fast save and share from your hand. The large-screen edition is for checking colour on a larger preview and finishing from one workspace.

No account is required for the main workflow. Open media, decide the colour direction, compare the result, then save, share, or export to a file.
